**Prepared:** 24 Nov 2026
**Data:** DataForSEO Labs (Google): keyword overview for India and the US, plus keyword suggestions. Volumes are 12-month averages covering Nov 2025 – Oct 2026.
**Builds on:** `docs/RHINON_LABS_AUDIT.md` (H-03 positioning, H-13 US page, H-11 local) and `docs/COMPETITOR_AILOITTE.md`.
**Scope:**
- ~360 seed keyword–market pairs (186 India, 171 US).
- ~650 long-tail suggestions for "ai automation", "mvp development" and "n8n".

---

## 1. Objective

Find the search terms that:
1. match what Rhinon Labs actually sells (AI agents, workflow automation, internal tools, MVPs, web and mobile apps);
2. carry buyer intent;
3. a brand-new domain can realistically rank for.

Then map each term to one page.

## 2. Current situation

Facts from the 23 Nov audit:
- **Zero rankings** in the top 100 in both markets.
- **No quality backlinks:** 28 domains link to the site, all spam.
- **Homepage title:** "Custom AI Automation & Internal Dashboards".
- **Demand for the homepage terms:**
  - "custom dashboard development" gets **10 searches/month** in India and 10 in the US.
  - "internal tools development" gets 10 in India.
  - Nobody searches the way the homepage currently talks.

**What follows from this:** keyword choice has to favour **low-difficulty, high-intent terms first**. Head terms come later, once links exist (C-05).

## 4. How keywords were prioritised

Each keyword was scored on four things:

| Factor | High | Medium | Low |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Business fit** | A service you sell today, with proof | Adjacent; needs a business decision | Not your market |
| **Intent** | Commercial or transactional (hiring a vendor) | Comparison or cost research | Learning, jobs, tools, brand |
| **Winnability for this domain** (DataForSEO KD 0–100) | KD ≤ 15 | KD 16–30 | KD > 30, or top-10 pages average 40+ linking domains |
| **Value** (US CPC as a proxy for deal value) | > $40 | $15–40 | < $15 |

The tiers that come out of this:
- **Tier 1:** build or fix now.
- **Tier 2:** months 3–9, needs some links.
- **Tier 3:** long-term head terms.
- **Avoid.**

**How to read the tables below**

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Vol | Average monthly searches over 12 months |
| KD | DataForSEO keyword difficulty |
| CPC | Google Ads top-of-page estimate, in USD |
| Trend | Year-on-year change |
| "—" | Not computed; usually a low-data term |
| "spiky" | The 12-month average hides big swings; don't plan traffic on it |

Volumes for close variants (for example "agency" and "agencies") come from the same Google bucket, so the cluster totals count each variant group once.

## 13. Data notes and gaps

**Notes on the data:**
- Data comes from DataForSEO Labs (Google India and US, English), pulled 24 Nov 2026.
- Volumes are averages over Nov 2025 – Oct 2026, in Google's bucketed figures (590, 720, 880…), so small differences don't mean anything.

- Intent labels come from DataForSEO's classifier.
