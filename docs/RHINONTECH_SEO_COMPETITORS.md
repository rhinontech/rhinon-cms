# Rhinon Tech: the six SEO competitors to benchmark

**Date:** 25 November 2026
**Question:** which 5–6 companies sell features like Rhinon Tech's *and* rank very well on Google?
**Scope:** the product sold on rhinontech.com, which has 11 modules in three groups (Sell, Deliver, People; see `rhinontech-site/content/products.ts`). Markets: Google India (primary) and Google US.
**Sources:**
- DataForSEO's stored Google data for India and the US (Labs API, November 2026) and the DataForSEO Backlinks API.
- Each vendor's own website, read on 25 November 2026.

Traffic figures are DataForSEO's estimates of monthly visits from Google's organic results. Use them to compare companies, not as exact counts.

How to read the table:
- **Non-brand** leaves out searches that contain the company's own name, such as "zoho mail login". It shows what the company earns from SEO, not from its brand.
- **Rhinon's buyer searches** are the 148 India and 126 US searches someone types when shopping for one of Rhinon's modules (Appendix B).

- **Half points** mean the vendor sells a partial version of a module (Appendix A).

What it means:
1. **Zoho matters most.** It sells 10 of the 11 modules. It is in Google India's top 10 for 39 of Rhinon's 148 buyer searches, covering 10 of the 11 modules (all except Performance) plus the all-in-one searches. The next vendor has 16. In India, Rhinon will meet Zoho on almost every search.
2. **HubSpot and Salesforce own the Sell searches** (CRM, chatbot, help desk, outreach) in both countries. Salesforce is #1 for "crm" and "crm software" in India and in the US.
3. **Odoo is the closest product after Zoho, but not a keyword rival.** It sells 8 of 11 modules and has the second-highest authority of the 50 vendors measured. Yet it reaches the top 10 on only 2 of Rhinon's buyer searches, both CRM.
4. **greytHR shows what a smaller Indian company can do.** It has 3,183 referring domains, about 1/40th of Zoho's. Even so, it is #2 for "payroll software" and #3 for "attendance software" in India.
5. **monday.com ranks with "best X software" blog lists.** One example: a blog post at #5 for "crm software" in the US, a search with 201,000 searches a month.
6. **rhinontech.com doesn't rank for any of these searches yet.** DataForSEO shows 0 keywords for it in India or the US, and it has 53 referring domains. It won't beat these six on head terms like "crm software" within a year. Use them as models to copy and names to track. The first wins will come from long-tail searches in India, where small vendors already reach the top 10 (section 5).

Keka, Bitrix24 and ClickUp came close (section 3).

## 1. How the six were picked

1. **Buyer searches.** I built 126 commercial searches covering Rhinon's 11 modules and the "all-in-one" angle. India got 22 more India-specific versions, such as "payroll software india" (Appendix B).
2. **Who ranks for them.** DataForSEO found 2,101 ranking domains in India and 3,012 in the US. I analysed the top 300 in each and set aside publishers and review sites: G2, Capterra, Forbes, Zapier, Reddit, YouTube, Wikipedia and government portals.
3. **SEO strength of 50 software vendors.** For each: total and non-brand Google traffic, keywords in the top 10, referring domains and authority (Appendix C).
4. **Feature overlap, checked on each vendor's own site.** Each of Rhinon's 11 modules scores 1 if the vendor sells it and 0.5 if it sells a partial version (Appendix A).
5. **Score.** Half is feature overlap. The other half is SEO strength: the average of non-brand traffic, top-10 spots on Rhinon's buyer searches, and authority, each scaled from 0 to 1.

## 2. The six in detail

### 1. Zoho (zoho.com): the all-in-one rival

**What it sells that Rhinon also sells: 10 of 11.**
- Sell: Zoho CRM, TeamInbox and Desk (shared inbox, help desk), and SalesIQ (live chat, chatbot).
- Deliver: Projects, Cliq (team chat), and Writer and WorkDrive (docs).
- People: Zoho People (records, onboarding, "Offer letter with e-sign", attendance, leave, appraisals) and Zoho Payroll India (PF, ESI, TDS and PT across 28 states).
- Partial: sales sequences (Campaigns, Marketing Automation). Video meetings weren't named on the pages read.
- Zoho One has no free-forever plan, only a 30-day trial.

**SEO.**
- India: 2.08M visits a month, 764k of them non-brand. 8,976 keywords in the top 10, 7,794 of them non-brand.
- US: 471k visits a month, 180k non-brand.
- Top 10 for 39 of 148 India buyer searches and 21 of 126 US.

**How it wins.** Each buyer search gets its own landing page inside the product's folder, and some products have separate India folders (`/en-in/crm/`, `/in/payroll/`):

**What to copy.**
- Rhinon's product URLs are named after modules (`/product/hr`, `/product/attendance`), not after what buyers type. Zoho has one page per buyer search, such as "HR software for small businesses", "free payroll software" and "attendance management system".
- Zoho also has "alternative" pages that target rivals' names.
- Two reference libraries link into those pages: an HR glossary (`/people/hrknowledgehive/`) and a payroll academy (`/in/payroll/academy/`).

### 2. Odoo (odoo.com): the closest product twin, and a benchmark for authority

**What it sells that Rhinon also sells: 8 of 11.**
- Full: CRM, Live Chat and Helpdesk, Project, Knowledge and Documents, Payroll.
- Partial:
  - Email Marketing, with no sequences;
  - Discuss for chat, with no video named;
  - Employees and Sign, with onboarding and offer letters not named;
  - Time Off;
  - Appraisals.
- Like Rhinon, it also has Events and e-signing.
- Plans: a free trial and an open-source edition.

**SEO.**
- Authority 91, second-highest of the 50 vendors measured, with 154,452 referring domains.
- India: 169k visits a month, 87k non-brand. US: 113k, 99k non-brand.
- On Rhinon's buyer searches it reaches the top 10 only for "free crm" and "free crm software" in India, both with odoo.com/app/crm. It has no top-10 spot on the 126 US searches, so its non-brand traffic comes from searches outside the ones tracked here.

**How it wins.**
- Authority built by its open-source community, partner network and app store.
- One page per app, such as odoo.com/app/crm.

**What to copy.**
- Copy the one-page-per-module structure and the "free CRM" angle, which Rhinon's Free plan competes with directly.
- Don't try to copy its link profile. That comes from being open source.

## 5. Where Rhinon starts, and what it can win

**Today**
- rhinontech.com: no keywords in Google's India or US top 100 (DataForSEO), 53 referring domains, authority 12.
- rhinontech.in (the app): 29 referring domains.
- A technical problem found while writing this report: see step 1 in section 6.

**Not winnable in the next 12 months:** head terms held by companies with 80k–400k referring domains.
- "crm": 135,000 searches a month in India, 165,000 in the US.
- "crm software": 74,000 in India, 201,000 in the US.
- "hr software": 12,100 in India.
- "project management software".

**Winnable first: India long-tail searches with weak top 10s.** Small vendors already reach the top 10:
- **"crm software india"** (480 a month):
  - #4 digisme.in, with 196 referring domains;
  - #5 groweon.com;
  - #6 leadsquared.com, with 4,816;
  - #7 peppercloud.com.
- **"hrms software"** (12,100 a month): #1 uknowva.com with 776 referring domains, #4 prajjo.com, #6 digisme.in.
- **"leave management system"** (2,400 a month): #2 digisme.in, #4 officetimer.com, #5 mewurk.com.

So in India, a well-targeted page can reach the top 10 with a few hundred referring domains.

**Don't target these yet**
- **Payroll searches** such as "payroll software" and "payroll software india".
  - greytHR, Zoho, Razorpay, Darwinbox and Keka hold them, and these buyers expect statutory compliance.
  - The launch review (`docs/ADMIN_PANEL_LAUNCH_REVIEW.md`, G3.1) found that Rhinon's TDS is a manual amount, professional tax is a flat ₹200, and ESI isn't calculated.
  - Ranking for these searches would bring buyers the product can't serve.
  - Target "HR software for small business", onboarding, and offer-letter e-signing instead.
- **"hrms"** (2.74M searches a month) is mostly people looking for a government or employer HR portal. gov.in and nic.in sites lead it. These are not buyers.
- **"crm" and other one-word terms.** People searching them want a definition, and Salesforce, Zoho and HubSpot own them.

## 7. Limits of this analysis

- **The same-day Google check failed.** DataForSEO's live Google results on 25 November came back degraded:
  - For "crm software" in India, positions 1–8 were a Play Store app and small blogs, with links wrapped in google.com redirects.
  - The same page's AI Overview cited Salesforce, Zoho and G2.
  - "project management software" in the US looked the same.
  - So every ranking in this report comes from DataForSEO's stored Google data. The AI Overview citations matched it.
- **Traffic is modelled.** DataForSEO estimates visits from position and search volume. Compare companies with it; don't treat it as a count.
- **Some searches had no stored results** in one or both countries, for example "shared inbox", "team chat app", "helpdesk software" and "hrms software india". Inbox, Team Chat and Pages are therefore measured on fewer searches.
- **Positions can differ by a place or two between DataForSEO endpoints.** For example, Odoo is #4 in one and #6 in the other for "free crm" in India. The top-10 counts are the same in both.
- **Feature checks:**
  - They come from vendor pages read on 25 November.
  - Parts of the long HubSpot, monday.com and ClickUp pages were read through a summary.
  - Zoho's applications page returned a 404, so Zoho was checked on its Zoho One, all-products, People and Payroll pages instead.
  - Freshworks and Lark couldn't be read.
- **India volumes include searches for a specific site,** notably "hrms".
- **The score weights are a judgement call.** Section 1 shows how the ranking changes with them.
